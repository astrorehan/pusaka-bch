Get started

Wallet guideSet up your plan and use your wallet. Try the live demoFollow a real plan and its transactions.

Plan lifecycle

  1. Use your wallet. Set your heirs, shares, and three trusted contacts. Each payment you send restarts the waiting time.
  2. Open and review a claim. Anyone can open a claim after the waiting time. Two trusted contacts can open one sooner. During review, you can stop the claim.
  3. Pay the heirs. Once review ends, anyone can submit the payout when the plan covers the fee and every heir’s minimum payment.

Read the wallet architecture and plan format for the contract rules and on-chain state.
