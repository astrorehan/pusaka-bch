## 1. Architecture & Contract Summary

Stage 3 unifies Stage 1's partial-payment wallet semantics and long-lived receive inbox with Stage 2's BIP68 CSV claim review clock into a compact, cycle-free 3-contract architecture built with `Contract` + `TransactionBuilder`:

1. [`PusakaV2Plan.scash`](../../contract/contracts/PusakaV2Plan.scash) — `3,161 B` compiled debug bytecode (`120 B` constructor/script template; P2SH32):
   - Constructor: `(bytes32 planCategory, bytes20 ownerPkh, bytes anchorLockingBytecode)` (strictly immutable plan identity).
   - Holds the unique `minting` NFT (`planCategory || 0x02`) and all protected BCH directly.
   - Commits mutable state in a 51-byte NFT commitment (`version=0x02`, `status`, `round`, `approvalsMask`, `deadline`, `inactivityPeriod`, `policyHash=hash256(policyBytes)`). See [pusaka-v2-plan-format.md](../architecture/pusaka-v2-plan-format.md).
   - Enforces:
     - `deposit()`: permissionless inbox consolidation / top-up across all 4 states (`ACTIVE`, `REVIEW`, `PAID_OUT`, `CLOSED`) without altering `deadline`, `round`, `approvalsMask`, `policyHash`, or touching `PusakaV2Anchor`.
     - `send(recipientLock, amount)`: owner-directed partial or full spendable payment (`spendable = input0.value - 1000`), protected change returning to `PusakaV2Plan`, `tx.time >= 500000000` (`OP_CLTV` requiring non-final sequence), deadline advancement (`<= 4294967295`), **bounded owner-authorized fee funding** (`<= 10,000` sats from protected BCH above the `1,000`-sat carrier when ordinary inputs do not cover fees, restricting change to `<= extraIn` and `ownerLock` so a `600`-sat persistent owner auth coin can be returned intact), and **atomic cancellation** of pending `REVIEW` + burning `PusakaV2Anchor` in the same transaction.
     - `checkIn()` / `cancelClaim()`: fallback check-in (also reactivates `CLOSED -> ACTIVE`) and explicit Stop Claim during `REVIEW`; both burn `PusakaV2Anchor` atomically, increment `round`, wipe `approvalsMask`, advance `deadline` (`<= 4294967295`), and share the bounded owner-authorized fee rule.
     - `updatePolicy(newPolicyBytes)`: edits 1–10 heirs/shares, 3 distinct contacts (`!= ownerPkh`), positive uint31 `inactivityPeriod`, `reviewSequence`, and `feeBudget`, advancing `round`, renewing `deadline` (`<= 4294967295`), wiping old `approvalsMask`, burning any pending `PusakaV2Anchor`, and supporting bounded owner-authorized fees while **never altering `planAddress` or `inboxAddress`**.
     - `ownerExit()`: requires `tx.time >= 500000000`, withdraws `planSats - 1000` to `ownerPkh`, renews `deadline = max(curDeadline, tx.locktime + period)` (`<= 4294967295`), clears `approvalsMask`, increments `round`, and enters `CLOSED (0x03)` (a withdrawn/retired balance state), retaining the `1,000`-sat `minting`-NFT identity carrier so future receipts at `inboxAddress` remain recoverable by the owner AND claimable by heirs.
     - `contactApprove(contactIndex, policyBytes)`: validates `hash256(policyBytes) == policyHash` and distinct contact PKHs across both `ACTIVE` and funded `CLOSED` (`input0.value > 1000` required when in `CLOSED`); first registered contact sets bit `1 << contactIndex` while preserving `ACTIVE` or `CLOSED`; second **distinct** registered contact transitions `ACTIVE`/`CLOSED -> REVIEW`, increments `round`, clears `approvalsMask`, and mints a fresh `PusakaV2Anchor` (`>= 1,000` sats, funded by the contact so contacts cannot drain plan funds). Contacts cannot spend, change policy, renew `deadline`, vote twice, or replay prior rounds.
     - `openClaimByInactivity(policyBytes)`: permissionless trigger across `ACTIVE` and funded `CLOSED` (`input0.value > 1000`) once `tx.time >= deadline` (`OP_CLTV`); mints a fresh `PusakaV2Anchor` starting its BIP68 review delay from 0s; self-funded path strictly requires `tx.outputs.length == 2` so callers cannot siphon the fee reserve.
     - `finalizeClaim(policyBytes)`: requires `tx.version >= 2`, `status == REVIEW`, matching `anchorUtxo` (`roundBytes || seqBytes`), enforces `expected_i = (pot * share_i) / totalShares >= 546` for 1–10 heirs, preserves rounding `leftover` + `1,000`-sat carrier in `PAID_OUT (0x02)` (plus unused fee budget in self-funded mode), and forbids caller change on self-funded calls (`1 + heirCount` outputs).
     - `distributeLate(policyBytes)`: permissionless distribution of post-payout receipts swept into `PAID_OUT` plans using the same integer share math, rounding remainder conservation, and self-funded output cap.

2. [`PusakaV2Anchor.scash`](../../contract/contracts/PusakaV2Anchor.scash) — `129 B` compiled debug bytecode (P2SH32):
   - Constructor: `(bytes32 planCategory, bytes20 ownerPkh)`.
   - Reads `reviewSequence` from its 8-byte `none`-NFT commitment (`bytes4(round) || bytes4(reviewSequence)`) and enforces `tx.version >= 2` + `this.age >= reviewSequence` (`OP_CHECKSEQUENCEVERIFY`) for `finalize()`, or owner P2PKH co-spend for instant `cancel()`.

3. [`PusakaV2Inbox.scash`](../../contract/contracts/PusakaV2Inbox.scash) — `261 B` compiled debug bytecode (P2SH32):
   - Constructor: `(bytes32 planCategory, bytes planLockingBytecode, int perInputFeeCap)`.
   - Provides a single unchanging receive address across the lifetime of the plan and sweeps `1..20` (`MAX_INBOX_SWEEP_INPUTS = 20`) plain-BCH UTXOs into `PusakaV2Plan` via either permissionless self-sweep (`feeAllowance = 3300 + inboxCount * perInputFeeCap`, protecting 100% of existing Plan value) or sponsored sweep (preserving 100% of `plan + inbox`).

Every Stage-3 input remains well below BCH 2026 consensus limits (`maximumInputBytes: 10,000`, `maximumStackItemBytes: 10,000`, `maximumNftCommitmentBytes: 128`, `maximumTransactionBytes: 1,000,000`):

## 3. Adversarial & Lifecycle Coverage (`PusakaV2.test.ts` & `PusakaV2Review.test.ts`)

1. **Full lifecycle + strict `Signer.external` + Faraid + policy edits + fresh-session attach**: verifies `ensureGenesisUtxo`, 6-heir Faraid resultant split, token-junk-resilient inbox self/sponsored sweeps leaving `deadline` unchanged, `withFundedPlainUtxoPreference` + `Signer.external` partial sends, `WcTransactionObject` `< 39 KB` serialization, `updatePolicy` preserving `inboxAddress` while invalidating old contact approvals & stale metadata links (`PolicyHashMismatchError`), 2-of-3 contact quorum, mature payout, and post-payout `distributeLate`.
2. **Maximum 10-heir family + VM headroom + rounding & dust rejection**: tests 10-heir custom percentage splits, asserts exact consensus byte limits and `getVmResourceUsage()` op-cost margins, verifies exact integer remainder conservation inside `PAID_OUT`, and proves sub-`546` heir outputs reject both off-chain and on-chain (`heir share below dust`).
3. **Full consensus BIP68 enforcement on BOTH triggers**: proves contact quorum AND inactivity trigger both enforce full time-based BIP68 delay from a freshly minted anchor (`0s`, unconfirmed `height=0`, `delay-1s`, `5`-block MTP stagnation, `tx.version=1`, disabled flag, height sequence, shorter sequence, and pre-deadline backdated `tx.locktime` all rejected).
4. **Owner atomic cancel across `send` / `cancelClaim` / `updatePolicy` + full drain + `CLOSED` exit & post-exit recovery**: verifies single-tx atomic cancel + anchor burn during `REVIEW`, blocks `ownerExit()` while in `REVIEW`, verifies `ownerExit` CLTV timestamp authentication & deadline renewal, full spendable drain to `1,000`-sat carrier + inbox revival, and proves post-`ownerExit` receipts sweep into `CLOSED` (without renewing deadline) and are recoverable by the owner.

7. **Post-review regressions (`PusakaV2Review.test.ts`, `7/7` + `PusakaV2Metadata.test.ts`, `1/1`)**: proves post-`ownerExit` `CLOSED` receipts remain claimable via BOTH renewed inactivity (`openClaimByInactivity`) AND 2-contact quorum (1st vote preserves `CLOSED`; sweeps preserve mask/deadline; 2nd vote/inactivity mints fresh Anchor with full BIP68 delay; empty `1,000`-sat `CLOSED` plan rejects claim entry; `ownerExit` enforces CLTV & `<= 0xffffffff` deadline); proves a single `600`-sat persistent owner auth coin repeatedly performs `send`, `checkIn`, `updatePolicy`, `send`-during-`REVIEW`, and `cancelClaim` via `Signer.external` with zero top-ups; verifies multi-UTXO `ownerUtxos`, `auto`/`owner`/`protected`/hybrid fee modes, rejection of `> 10,000`-sat fee, `< 1,000`-sat carrier breach, payee/change siphons, non-owner protected spends, tampered `reviewUnits`/`effectiveReviewSeconds`/network metadata, `20`-UTXO sweep cap, and 10-heir policy updates during `REVIEW`.
