## 1. Pusaka

Use your money today. Leave what remains to your family.

Bitcoin Cash inheritance with a usable balance and time to stop a mistaken claim.
Demo: https://astrorehan.github.io/pusaka-bch/wallet/demo
Code: https://github.com/astrorehan/pusaka-bch

## 2. Money that remains useful

A separate locked savings pot makes the owner repeatedly allocate funds.
Pusaka attaches family rules to a balance the owner can spend. Owner payments renew
activity. New receipts stay protected without becoming a false proof of life.
Onboarding includes a new address and an initial transfer.

## 3. The wallet

Show the actual Wallet screen. BCH Send keeps protected change. Receive has a
permanent inbox. The wallet reviews fees and carrier values before asking Paytaca
to approve. The fallback check-in remains available.

## 4. Family rules

One to ten heirs, equal or integer custom shares, and the existing common-family
faraid calculator. Three trusted contacts, with two distinct approvals opening
review. Contacts cannot spend or change the policy. Inactivity offers a separate
claim route. Policy updates invalidate earlier approvals.

## 5. Time to stop a false claim

The network starts a fresh BIP68 anchor when review begins. An owner action can
cancel that anchor. Deposits cannot renew life or shorten the review clock.
The Chipnet node rejected immediate finalization and accepted the mature claim.
The demonstration uses 512 seconds; new UI plans default to at least seven days.

## 6. Trading proceeds stay covered

The BCH / PSK Chipnet pair uses the actual Cauldron V0 script. The owner signs one
transaction that recreates the pool, renews activity and pays tokens directly into
the Pusaka token vault. PSK is a test token with no monetary value. Mainnet pair
qualification remains open. Arbitrary DeFi positions are outside this integration.

## 7. One family, exact token conservation

Live Chipnet swap return: 199,519 fungible units. After the BCH claim matured,
the token payout sent 66,506, 66,506 and 66,507 units to the three configured heirs.
Each received an 800-sat carrier. Network fee: 5,190 sats. The final heir receives
indivisible rounding units. Show the raw transaction receipt, not an invented chart.

## 9. Work before and during BLAZE

The earlier Monad product supplied the inheritance concept, share calculator and
flower identity. Its EIP-7702 account delegation does not exist on BCH.
The BCH work uses explicit covenant addresses, a state NFT, the receive inbox,
BIP68 claim anchor and the asset vault. The browser interface and builders expose
that construction. Existing ordinary wallet and exchange balances stay separate.
