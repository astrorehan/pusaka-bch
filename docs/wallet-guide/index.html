Live app: https://astrorehan.github.io/pusaka-bch/ (mainnet) and https://astrorehan.github.io/pusaka-bch/chipnet/ (Chipnet, the test network).

The demo at /wallet/demo opens a live example plan whose owner has gone quiet. Any visitor can open its claim or pay its heirs, with no wallet of their own: the plan pays the network fee. Create your own wallet to try the owner's actions.

Owner

  1. Open Wallet, choose Set up wallet, and connect Paytaca.
  2. Add heirs and choose equal shares, integer custom shares or the existing faraid family calculator. Verify addresses and the calculation before approving.
  3. Add three distinct trusted contacts. Choose inactivity and review periods.
  4. Enter an initial usable BCH balance. This creates a new Pusaka receive address. Keep at least 600 sats at the first ordinary wallet receive address for authorization.
  5. If necessary, use Prepare wallet, review that separate transaction, then review wallet creation. Approve each in Paytaca. Save the resulting plan JSON/link.
  6. Use Send for partial BCH payments. Protected change remains in Pusaka and the same transaction renews activity. I'm still here is the fallback.
  7. Receive shows the permanent BCH inbox. Incoming funds are protected without owner approval. Collect makes batched receipts spendable and charges the reviewed network fee. Incoming receipts never renew activity.
  8. During review, Stop claim or an ordinary BCH payment cancels the claim. An owner token send and Swap currently ask you to stop a pending claim first.
  9. Take funds back retires the current BCH balance. The old receive address still protects later receipts. Token holdings stay under the plan's family rules.

Family

Open the shared plan link or import its JSON. A contact connects their own wallet and chooses Confirm a claim. The first approval has no spending authority. The second distinct contact opens the full review period. Alternatively, anyone can open an inactivity claim after the network deadline.

Wait for the anchor to confirm and its chain age to mature. The network clock can lag behind a phone. Pay the heirs submits the fixed BCH distribution. Afterward, Pay late receipts handles new BCH. Under Protected tokens, Pay heirs distributes one fungible receipt per transaction; the submitter provides BCH for token carriers and fees. All indivisible units go to the final heir.

Swap

On Chipnet, open Swap for BCH / PSK. PSK is a test token, not an investment. Get a quote, inspect minimum return and fees, then review the complete transaction. The token output goes directly into your Pusaka token vault. Use that token address only for supported fungible tokens. The BCH inbox is a different address.

After reconnecting

Open the saved plan link or choose Open a plan and paste JSON. Public rules rebuild the same addresses, and the app discovers current state from the chain. Reconnect the correct wallet for owner or contact actions. Save an updated link after policy edits; old policy data fails closed instead of redirecting payouts.

An accepted transaction still needs block confirmation. If submission times out, check its transaction ID and refresh before trying again. The local activity list records submissions from that browser, not a complete recovered chain history.

Demo narration

Show the usable BCH balance, a partial payment renewing activity and a new receipt. Show two contacts opening review and the owner stopping a false claim. Then show a valid mature claim and the fixed multi-heir payout. Finish with the Cauldron swap and the token payout receipts in the verification record.

The 512-second Chipnet review is a test configuration. New UI plans default to 90 days of inactivity and at least 7 days of review. Explain the initial move to a new address. Do not promise inheritance for untouched Paytaca or exchange funds.

The real phone/mainnet checks still pending are listed in the release evidence.
